About Me

I’m Shouren Wang, a PhD student in Computer Science at Case Western Reserve University, advised by Prof. Xiaotian Han and Prof. Vipin Chaudhary. I am a member of TAPP AI.

Previously, I earned an M.S. in Computer Engineering from New York University, where I was part of the Game Innovation Lab, and a B.E. in Digital Media Technology from Hunan University.

I am actively seeking internship opportunities and would be glad to discuss potential research collaborations.

Research Interests

My current work broadly concerns large language model reasoning, post-training, efficient language models, and AI agent systems.
